Summary

Interests

My research interests span infectious disease, social, spatial, genomic, and wastewater-based epidemiology; biological, medical, and sociocultural anthropology; Caribbean archaeology and heritage management; and agriculture and food science. I am particularly interested in interdisciplinary, mixed-methods approaches integrating computational, laboratory, field-based, and archival research to investigate the biological, environmental, historical, and sociocultural dimensions of health and disease.

Experience

My experience encompasses fieldwork, laboratory research, scientific writing, data analysis, curation, conservation, and project management. Ultimately, I hope to pursue doctoral research in Epidemiology, Environmental Health Sciences, or a related field, advancing historically informed and equity-centered planetary health research across the Caribbean and Americas.
